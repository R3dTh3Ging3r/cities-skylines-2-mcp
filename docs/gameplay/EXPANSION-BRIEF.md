## Decisions to resolve after research

1. Which east–west street best serves both districts while leaving adequate space from the northern interchange? Survey Highland and Arborview as candidates and compare alternatives; do not choose by screenshot alone.
2. Does a compact diamond fit better than a diamond with roundabout terminals? Include approach lengths and terminal storage in both footprints. Native presets are candidates, not guaranteed matches.
3. Which additional local road/pedestrian crossings should remain without freeway ramps? Maintain practical access between both districts during and after construction.

5. Which initial housing blocks can operate with existing utilities/services and which trigger recurring-cost increases? Plan the whole district, fund and grow it in manageable phases.
